# Sanjar Osmanov

Student developer from Turkmenistan interested in robotics, aerospace, AI engineering, and scientific research. I spend most of my time building things, testing ideas, and learning how the underlying systems work.

## Projects

### Darvaza Watch
A satellite-based monitoring project focused on the Darvaza gas crater in Turkmenistan. The project brings together a public observatory dashboard and an automated data pipeline. Current work includes satellite scene discovery and geospatial analysis using Landsat and Sentinel data. The processing and validation work is still in progress.

[View the project](https://github.com/Emir731/Darvaza-Data)

### HyperAI
An offline AI assistant built with Flutter. The goal is to make text and image-based help for subjects such as mathematics, physics, geometry, and chemistry available on mobile devices without an internet connection. The project is being developed with multilingual support in mind.

[View the repository](https://github.com/Emir731/HyperAI)

### Robotics and embedded systems
I've built and tested several electronics and robotics projects, including an Arduino robot kit, a line-following robot, an AI-camera parking robot, and a radar project. I have also taken part in robotics programs and competitions in Turkmenistan.

### Helicopter and drone projects
I designed a full-size helicopter concept and built a smaller prototype. I also built a working drone. The full-size helicopter has not been flight-tested because of local flight restrictions.

### Hyperia Discord bot
I created Hyperia in 2021 and developed it into a Discord bot used across more than 50 servers, with over 200,000 users and 110+ commands at its peak. The original source code is no longer available.

## Independent research

I work on research questions in resistance training and muscle hypertrophy. My two current projects examine training volume and the effects of training to failure compared with stopping sets with repetitions in reserve.

- [Training Volume and Hypertrophy](https://github.com/Emir731/training-volume-hypertrophy)
- [Training to Failure vs. Repetitions in Reserve](https://github.com/Emir731/training-failure-vs-rir-hypertrophy)

Both repositories include research notes, extracted data, and analysis code. The projects are ongoing, and I document limitations and unverified evidence rather than presenting preliminary results as settled conclusions.
